## Overview

**Tirana Home Finder** is an automated residential rental alert service that monitors major Albanian real-estate agencies and classifieds portals twice daily, delivering verified long-term apartments and houses in Tirana within budget (`<= 400 EUR / month`) straight to Telegram.

## What It Delivers

- **Multi-Portal Coverage in One Feed:** Consolidates new Tirana rental listings across 8 major local property platforms so you never miss affordable apartments before they get taken.
- **Standardized EUR & ALL Pricing:** Automatically converts and displays every listing in both **EUR** and **ALL** using the official Bank of Albania exchange rate for effortless comparison.
- **Clean Residential Filtering:** Excludes daily/short-stay rentals, offices, shops, garages, and ambiguous or outdated listings — sending only verified monthly residential homes and apartments.
- **Morning & Evening Digests:** Dispatched at `09:00` and `21:00` Tirana time with zero duplicate alerts.
